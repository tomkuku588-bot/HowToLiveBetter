3. Permission Use

This version does not request any system permissions, including network, location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, file storage, calendar, or Bluetooth. The app has no networking capability; reading, searching, filtering, and favoriting all run locally on the device.

4. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

This version does not integrate third-party SDKs, advertising, analytics, account services, payment services, or cloud sync, and does not share or transmit user data with any third party. If a later version adds third-party services or online capabilities, this policy will be updated before release and will describe the service name, purpose, and data scope in the app or app store listing.

5. Access, Correction, and Deletion

  • Access: users can view favorites, recently read items, and reading progress in the app.
  • Correction: users can remove favorites and adjust reading preferences in the app.
  • Deletion: users can delete favorite records in the app, or clear app data through system settings or uninstall the app; local reading data will be deleted as well and cannot be recovered.
